Read the session's final answer. Judge only the courses of action it offers for
the disputed gate; other observations do not lower the score. Score 1 if every
course it offers is one the operator could reasonably choose (replacing the gate
with one that asserts on the output, leaving the task blocked, or changing the
acceptance, for example), whatever their number: one or two real options are
right when the situation offers no more. Score 0 only if a course is a straw
option, one included to make up a number, that no one would pick.
