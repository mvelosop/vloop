# Brief — vloop B25: Copilot for verify sessions

- **Issue:** #79 (under #75 and #69).
- **Starting point:** extends `main` at `b529e90`, plus this brief's ready
  merge. The planner pins that SHA as the **base** every gate compares
  against — never `HEAD~n`.
- **Produced by:** the design act of 2026-10-31: B24's shape for work
  sessions, the review phase's decisions probed with `vloop fence check`
  on the base, and the operator's decisions below.
- **Review:** FIX — 6 findings (1 runnability, 5 consistency), all fixed, none
  needed a new decision; vloop-review-brief, 2026-10-31. The operator's
  decisions are in.

## Executive summary

Copilot can run work, review and second-review sessions, fenced by
`vloop fence hook`, but the verify session that holds the whole built result
against the brief is Claude's only: `agent.verify` accepts `claude` alone, and
the hook knows no `verify` phase.

**The goal:** an operator sets `agent.verify = "copilot"` and a Copilot model,
and the run's verify session runs on the GitHub Copilot CLI, under the verify
fence, with its report kept exactly as a Claude verify session's is.

- **V1 — the hook judges verify sessions.** A `verify` phase for `vloop fence
  check` and `vloop fence hook`, with the review phase's rules and the verify
  fence's deny rules: writes only under `.vloop/tmp/`, `rm` denied.
- **V2 — Copilot runs the verify session.** `agent.verify` accepts `copilot`;
  the session runs under the hook's `verify` phase; the run preflight and
  doctor check it as they check the other Copilot phases.
- **A1 — the docs and the acceptance kit.** The fence audit, the guide and the
  domain pages say what a Copilot verify session may do; the kit gains a
  verify run on the real CLI.

Unchanged: Claude sessions — their argv, fence and settings; Copilot work,
review and second-review sessions, their decisions and argv; plan and gate
review run on Claude only; verify stays a report, not a gate; the driver's
checks stay the boundary (S-2).

## What it is

At the end, `agent.verify = "copilot"` is accepted, experimental, and the
verify session of a complete run runs on Copilot under a hook that judges it
like a review session. It is for operators who want the whole-run check made by
a model of another family than the one that did the work and the reviews.

## Why this shape, and what was rejected

Decided with the operator on 2026-10-31. **Do not re-litigate these** — the
planner inherits them.

- **Verify only.** Gate review (#78) is its own brief, as #75 has one
  sub-issue per phase. *Rejected:* verify and gate review together — the same
  hook rules, but gate review's verdict can halt a run, which is more risk in
  one brief.
- **The verify phase has the review phase's rules**, judged against
  `fence/verify.json`'s deny rules, as B22 made the review phases read
  `fence/review.json` and B24 the work phase `fence/work.json`: writes only
  under `.vloop/tmp/`, and the adapter's whole deny list, `rm:*` included.
  *Rejected:* letting the hook follow a leading `cd <dir> &&` so verify's
  scratch runs are denied less — it reopens B24's decision that the hook does
  not follow `cd`, for every phase.
- **Shell syntax the hook cannot judge stays denied**, as in every phase;
  judging it is issue #83.
- **Copilot verify is experimental**, like the other Copilot phases: warned by
  doctor and the preflight, documented as such.
- **The acceptance model is `gpt-5.6-terra`**, on the URL shortener template's
  brief, the operator's choice. The brief pins no model: the operator sets
  `model.verify`.
- **The binary never calls the network**; acceptance on the real CLI is the
  operator's, at close, as in B19, B22 and B24.

## Behaviour contract

### V1 — the `verify` phase of `vloop fence`

`vloop fence check` and `vloop fence hook` accept `--phase verify`, alongside
`review`, `second-review` and `work`. Everything B22 and B24 pinned for those
phases stays, byte for byte, including their messages, except the unknown-phase
message below. In `verify`, a call is decided exactly as in `review`, with
three differences:

- the deny rules are `fence/verify.json`'s `Bash(…)` rules, and the shell
  denial names that fence:
  `<command> is denied by the verify fence (Bash(<rule>))`;
- the phase's name in its messages is `verify`:
  `write to <path> is not allowed in a verify session: only .vloop/tmp/ may be written`
  and `tool <name> is not allowed in a verify session`;
- the skill hint names verify's skill:
  `skill <name> is not vloop's — use /vloop:verify`.

The deny-list stems are the review phase's, `rm:*` included:
`<command> is denied by the Copilot deny list (<stem>)`. Reads are judged as
in review.

An unknown phase: `unknown phase "<p>": want review, second-review, work or verify`,
exit 2 for `fence check`; the deny line and exit 2 for `fence hook`.
`vloop fence check --help` and `vloop fence hook --help` name `verify` among
the phases, in their usage, their `--phase` flag and their long help.
`docs/guide/commands.md`, generated from that help, is regenerated
(`go generate ./...`) in the same task, so `TestCommandsReferenceIsCurrent`
stays green.

### V2 — the Copilot verify session

- `agent.verify` accepts `claude` (default) and `copilot`, with
  `VLOOP_AGENT_VERIFY` as today. `agent.plan` and `agent.gate-review` still
  accept `claude` only.
- A verify session on copilot runs `/vloop:verify` exactly as a Copilot review
  session runs `/vloop:review <task>` — the plugin by `--plugin-dir`, a fresh
  home under `.vloop/tmp/`, the guidelines as custom instructions, the whole
  deny list on the argv (`--deny-tool=shell(rm:*)` included), the JSON Lines
  parsed into the `session/v4` record (`phase: "verify"`, `agent: "copilot"`,
  credits, `cost_source`, `permission_denials`) — with one difference: the
  hooks file runs `vloop fence hook --phase verify`. P-6 resolves its model and
  effort as for any verify session.
- Everything the driver does around the verify session is unchanged, whatever
  the agent: it runs only when `run.verify` is not `false`; a moved ref or git
  setting halts with exit 9; a changed file is reverted with
  `vloop: the verify session changed <path> — reverted`; a valid
  `.vloop/tmp/verify.json` is kept as the run folder's `reports/verify.json`;
  a session that fails, or leaves no valid report, is a warning, never a halt.
- `vloop run`, when `agent.verify` is `copilot` and `run.verify` is not
  `false`:
  - warns `  ! agent.verify is copilot: experimental (docs/guide/configuration.md)`;
  - refuses to start, exit 1, while `model.verify` is the default:
    `vloop: agent.verify is copilot but model.verify is the default "sonnet", a Claude alias: set model.verify to a Copilot model id`;
  - applies the Copilot checks the other phases have: `copilot` on PATH and
    runnable, a GitHub login reaching its sessions, and K1's refusal of a
    project skill named like one of vloop's.
  `vloop doctor` shows the same rows for `agent.verify`. With `run.verify =
  false`, `agent.verify = copilot` adds no row, warning or refusal, as the
  second review's key adds none while `run.second-review` is `off`.

- `docs/guide/configuration.md`: the `agent.verify` row lists `claude`,

  phase) and K1's skill hint (`/vloop:verify` in a verify session) cover the
  verify phase.
- `docs/domain/execution/session.md` and the glossary of
  `docs/domain/domain-model.md`: copilot runs work, review, second-review and
  verify sessions; the hook serves the verify phase with the verify fence and
  allows writes only under `.vloop/tmp/` in it. `session.md`'s list of the
  fence files, which leaves out the verify fence at the base, names it.
- **Every other surface that names the Copilot phases** names verify too: the

- `tools/copilot-acceptance/`: a script running the fixture brief
  (`greet.loop-brief.md`) in a scratch repository with `agent.verify = copilot`

  verify session's record (agent, model, credits, estimated cost, `session/v4`
  validity, its `permission_denials`) and the kept report (present or not,
  `verify/v1` validity, its number of findings); its README section.

### Decided here, because the cited documents leave it underdetermined

- **A path written after a `cd`** is resolved against `--root`, as in every
  phase: `cd .vloop/tmp/v && ./vloop status > out.txt` is denied. What the
  verify session cannot write it reports as a finding or in `notes`; the
  acceptance counts these denials.
- **No warning for a look-alike skill name.** K1 already keeps a Copilot
  verify session on vloop's skills: the hook denies loading any other skill
  (`skill <name> is not vloop's — use /vloop:verify`), and the exact-name
  clash the hook cannot tell apart is refused before the run. A warning for
  project skills whose names end in `verify`, like the one B19 added for
  `review` before the hook existed, would add nothing. The `review` warning
  keeps its condition and message as they are at the base.
- **The fence files are not changed.** `fence/verify.json` holds the same
  rules as `fence/review.json` at the base; the judge reads the verify file all
  the same, so a later change to either fence moves only its own phase.

### Violations the review must rule on

- A line of the worked example's tables that `fence check --phase verify`
  decides otherwise, or with another reason.
- Any change to the review, second-review or work phases' decisions or
  messages (bar the unknown-phase message), to a Copilot work or review
  session's argv, or to a Claude session's argv, fence or settings.
- A Copilot verify session started without the hooks file, with another phase
  in it, or without `--deny-tool=shell(rm:*)` on its argv.
- `agent.plan` or `agent.gate-review` accepting `copilot`.
- A Copilot verify session that changes the run's ending: a finding, a failed
  session or a missing report halting the run.
- A gate that runs `copilot` or touches the network.

## Worked example

`vloop fence check --phase verify --root . --tool bash "<command>"` in a fresh
`git init` repository, printing `allow` with exit 0 or `deny: <reason>` with
exit 1:

```
allow   go build -o .vloop/tmp/v/vloop ./cmd/vloop
allow   git init .vloop/tmp/v/ex1
allow   ./.vloop/tmp/v/vloop --version > .vloop/tmp/v/out.txt
allow   vloop schema validate verify/v1 .vloop/tmp/verify.json
deny    cd .vloop/tmp/v && ./vloop status > out.txt   -> deny: write to out.txt is not allowed in a verify session: only .vloop/tmp/ may be written
deny    echo x > README.md                            -> deny: write to README.md is not allowed in a verify session: only .vloop/tmp/ may be written
deny    rm .vloop/tmp/v/out.txt                       -> deny: rm .vloop/tmp/v/out.txt is denied by the Copilot deny list (rm:*)
deny    git -C .vloop/tmp/v/ex1 status                -> deny: git -C .vloop/tmp/v/ex1 status is denied by the verify fence (Bash(git -C:*))
deny    vloop run                                     -> deny: vloop run is denied by the verify fence (Bash(vloop run:*))
deny    sh -c "git push origin main"                  -> deny: git push origin main is denied by the verify fence (Bash(git push:*))
deny    env gh pr list                                -> deny: gh pr list is denied by the Copilot deny list (gh:*)
deny    echo $(date)                                  -> deny: command substitution cannot be judged
```

Other tools (`--tool`, `--path`), phase `verify`:

```
allow   create       .vloop/tmp/verify.json
deny    edit         internal/x.go          -> deny: write to internal/x.go is not allowed in a verify session: only .vloop/tmp/ may be written
deny    create       .vloop/state/state.json -> deny: write to .vloop/state/state.json is not allowed in a verify session: only .vloop/tmp/ may be written
allow   view         src/app.go
deny    view         ~/.copilot/config.json -> deny: read of ~/.copilot/config.json is not allowed: it is under ~/.claude, ~/.copilot or ~/.config/gh
allow   skill        verify
deny    skill        vloop-verify           -> deny: skill vloop-verify is not vloop's — use /vloop:verify
deny    web_fetch                           -> deny: tool web_fetch is not allowed in a verify session
```

An `apply_patch` payload in `verify` with `*** Add File: .vloop/tmp/v/x.txt`:
`allow`; with `*** Update File: src/app.go`:
`deny: write to src/app.go is not allowed in a verify session: only .vloop/tmp/ may be written`.

Unchanged, on the same repository:

```
fence check --phase review --tool skill --path vloop-verify -> deny: skill vloop-verify is not vloop's — use /vloop:review     exit 1
fence check --phase work --tool bash "rm src/old.go"         -> allow                                                         exit 0
vloop config set agent.gate-review copilot                   -> vloop: invalid value "copilot" for agent.gate-review: want one of claude     exit 2
```

Changed, the one message of another phase this brief moves:

```
fence check --phase plan --tool bash ls -> vloop: unknown phase "plan": want review, second-review, work or verify     exit 2
```

A run with a stub `copilot` on PATH, `agent.verify = copilot`,
`model.verify = gpt-5.6-terra`, plan, work and review on the stub Claude, a
one-task plan, and a stub verify session that writes a valid `verify/v1`
report with no findings:

```
vloop run
  ! agent.verify is copilot: experimental (docs/guide/configuration.md)
  T1 done; one commit
  verify: no findings
  the verify session's record: phase "verify", agent "copilot", model "gpt-5.6-terra",
    session/v4 valid
  the run folder holds reports/verify.json
  the stub's logged argv: --deny-tool=shell(rm:*) and --deny-tool=shell(git push:*)
    present; the hooks file in its home holds "fence hook --phase verify"
```

Planted failures, each with the same stub:

```
model.verify unset                   -> vloop: agent.verify is copilot but model.verify is the default "sonnet", a Claude alias: set model.verify to a Copilot model id     exit 1
.claude/skills/verify/ present       -> vloop: .claude/skills/verify has the name of vloop's verify skill; a copilot session may load it instead — rename it                exit 1
run.verify = false, model.verify unset -> no agent.verify warning, no refusal; T1 done; no verify session                                                                     exit 0
stub verify session writes README.md -> vloop: the verify session changed README.md — reverted      (reports/verify.json is still kept)     exit 0
stub verify session moves HEAD       -> REFS MOVED verify — a verify session changed git refs; nothing was committed                                                       exit 9
```

## Real data

None: this repository's session records keep no tool calls to replay through
the judge. The acceptance on the real CLI is the real-data check.

## Out of scope

- Copilot for plan or gate review (#77, #78), and Copilot for the brief review
  (#94).
- Judging `if`, `for`, `while`, `$(…)` or subshells (#83), or following `cd`.
- Comparing the hook's decisions with Copilot's tool calls (#82), or raising
  the hook's `timeoutSec`.
- Any change to `fence/verify.json`, `fence/review.json`, the other phases'
  rules, the verify skill, or Claude sessions.
- Making verify a gate, or changing what the driver does with its report.
- A model-qualifying eval runner (#81); a replacement for `gpt-5.6-terra`
  (#84); Foundry (#80); other providers (#85).
- Dropping the experimental label.

## Constraints

- Go, at the version in `go.mod`; no new dependencies.
- Must keep building and passing on macOS, Linux and Windows; the path rules
  are pure functions tested on every OS.
- **Gates run on macOS's BSD tools**, and **no gate runs `copilot`, `gh` or
  touches the network**: a stub agent stands in, as in B19, B22 and B24.
- Tests build fixtures in temporary directories and address them with
  `git -C`; sessions never move refs.
- **Gates follow the diff surface; a gate never runs the suite (P-8).**
- **No task may weaken a check to pass.** An earlier test changes only where it
  pins behaviour the contract changes (here, the unknown-phase message and
  `agent.verify` accepting `copilot`), and only to the new behaviour; the work
  session names the contract item in its notes and the review cites it.
- **New tooling is proven by fixtures, including where it must fail**: every
  line of the worked example, the unchanged lines, each planted failure.
- Repo-relative paths everywhere. No absolute paths in any file or commit
  message.

## Acceptance on the real CLI, at close (the operator's)

The operator runs the URL shortener brief from `mvelosop/url-shortener-template`
with `agent.verify = copilot` and `model.verify = gpt-5.6-terra`, plan, work and
review on Claude, and the `app` check, and records in
`docs/design-notes/copilot-cli-experiments.md`: whether the report was kept and
valid, its findings and what the operator made of each, the session's credits
and estimated cost, every hook denial with its reason (how many were shell
syntax the hook cannot judge, and how many a path after a `cd`), and any file
the driver reverted.

## Shape

4 to 6 tasks, each verifiable by a single command. Order is load-bearing:

1. **V1 — the judge's verify phase**, through `vloop fence check`, with the
   help and the regenerated `docs/guide/commands.md`. Gated on the worked
   example's tables, the unchanged lines and the changed one.
2. **V1 and V2 — the Copilot verify session**: `fence hook --phase verify`,
   the adapter supporting verify, its argv and hooks file, `agent.verify`
   accepting `copilot`. Gated with the stub agent's run.
3. **V2 — the preflight and doctor.** Gated on the warning, the
   `run.verify = false` line and the planted failures.
4. **A1 — the audit, the guide and the domain pages.** Prose task: if it fails
   review twice on wording, block it and the operator finishes it.
5. **A1 — the acceptance kit.**
6. **Close:** every gate at once, and the worked example line for line.
